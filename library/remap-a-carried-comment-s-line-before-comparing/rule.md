# Remap a carried comment's line before comparing it at a newer head

A carried-forward review comment's original line belongs to its old commit, so clear or remap that line before comparing source at a newer head. Keep the earlier reviewed-head attribution when you do this. Add a test where the comment's updated line differs from its original line.
